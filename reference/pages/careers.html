About us

We are the frontier on-device AI lab.

We build the models, inference runtime, and quantization stack. From the device constraint up.

So AI can run at full capability on the hardware billions of people already own.

Why us?

Mirai is founded by proven entrepreneurs who built and scaled consumer AI leaders like Reface (300M users, backed by Andreessen Horowitz) and Prisma (100M MAU). Our team is small (16 people), senior, and deeply technical. We ship fast and own problems end-to-end.

We’re advised by a former Apple Distinguished Engineer who worked on MLX, and backed by leading AI-focused funds and individuals.
